Nigeria: Inflation and the Naira (2023–2026)
An Excel dashboard comparing Nigerian inflation (NBS CPI) with the naira’s monthly average exchange rate (CBN NFEM).
What’s inside: four charts, a year selector, and a cleaning log.
Data: NBS CPI exports (Nov 2023 to Nov 2025) and CBN NFEM daily rates (Dec 2024 to Nov 2026), turned into monthly averages.
Cleaning: removed 6 duplicate exchange-rate rows, converted text dates, and kept the old and new CPI bases separate after the 2025 rebase.
Limitations: only 12 months of overlap between the two datasets, so the dashboard shows how the two series moved but not whether one caused the other.
Tools: Built with Excel an limited AI assistance; all figures checked against the source files.
