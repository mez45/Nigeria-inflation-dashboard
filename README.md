Nigeria: Inflation and the Naira (2023–2026)
An Excel dashboard comparing Nigerian inflation (NBS CPI) with the naira's monthly average exchange rate (CBN NFEM).
![Dashboard](Dashboard%20NG.png)
 What's inside
- Four charts and a year selector
- A cleaning log tab documenting every step
 Data
- NBS CPI exports (Nov 2023 to Nov 2025)
- CBN NFEM daily exchange rates (Dec 2024 to Nov 2026), turned into monthly averages
 Cleaning
- Removed 6 duplicate exchange-rate rows
- Converted text dates to real dates
- Kept the old and new CPI bases separate after the 2025 rebase
Limitations
Only 12 months of overlap between the two datasets, so the dashboard shows how the two series moved but not whether one caused the other.
Tools
Built in Excel with Limited AI assistance. All figures were checked against the source files.
